Checklist

Pagination SEO checks before launch

This is a manual QA asset for launch owners. It is not a crawler, Search Console export, PageSpeed report, or indexing guarantee.

1. Crawlable links

Confirm page numbers, next links, and previous links are real anchor tags with `href` values. Avoid span-only, button-only, or click-handler-only controls for crawler discovery.

2. Unique page URLs

Verify page 2+ has stable URLs such as `?page=2` or `/page/2/`. Fragment-only states like `#page-3` are weak crawl targets.

3. Sequential coverage

Check that users and crawlers can move through adjacent pages. A jump from page 1 to page 5 with no next path can hide deeper content.

4. Canonicals

Review page 2+ canonical tags. Important paginated pages should not be accidentally canonicalized to page 1 when they contain distinct products, articles, docs, reviews, or comments.

5. Meta robots

Confirm `noindex` is intentional on page 2+ content. A copied template-level noindex can remove valuable archive or listing pages from consideration.

6. Load-more controls

If users click "Load more", provide crawlable fallback URLs. Button-only loading can look fine in QA while hiding deeper inventory from crawlers.

7. Infinite scroll

When infinite scroll is used, verify paginated URLs exist independently, content is reachable without scroll events, and internal links expose deeper states.

8. Sitemap fallback

Include representative important page 2+ URLs in sitemap samples when internal link discovery is fragile or launch templates are still changing.

9. Anchor labels

Replace empty or generic anchors with clear labels such as page number, next page, previous page, or older articles. This improves QA readability and handoff clarity.
